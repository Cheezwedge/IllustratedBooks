# 3 · Illustration plan

The editable source of truth is **`plan/illustrations.csv`**: open it in any spreadsheet. The build reads it directly, and this page is regenerated from it by `python3 tools/plan_report.py`.

## 3.1 How every page turn gets a picture

| Layer | Count | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cutaway spreads (S) | 9 | The showpieces: double-page cross-sections of the key places |
| Full-page plates (P) | 31 | One to three per chapter, at the big story moments |
| Half-page pictures (H) | 40 | The top of a text page, roughly every 6–8 pages |
| Inset spots (I) | 55 | Small pictures with text wrapping around them |
| Chapter openers (O) | 19 | A bramble banner with an emblem roundel, plus an illuminated drop cap (**all drafted**) |
| Tailpieces (V) | 20 | A bramble sprig closing each chapter (**drafted**) |
| Margin critters (M) | 13 drafted, aim for about 40 | Stamped automatically on any page that has no other art |
| Blank-page vignettes | automatic | Blank versos before chapter openings show the next chapter's emblem |

In the current dummy (407 pages), every page that has no planned art gets a margin critter (206 pages) and every blank page gets a vignette (11). So **no page is ever without a picture**. To make the critter pages richer, add more insets to the CSV; the layout absorbs them automatically.

**Totals:** 176 planned pieces (priority A: 77, B: 51, C: 48). 41 are already drafted as style studies in `art/png/`.

**Priorities:**

- **A** is essential for launch.
- **B** strongly wanted.
- **C** can be phased into a later printing; until then a margin critter takes its place.

## 3.2 The big cutaways

These are the pages people will buy the book for. Each one gets a full research pass: how the room would really be built, lit, heated and stocked.

- **S-01-01: Cutaway: the hobbit-hole.** Double-page cross-section of the hobbit-hole: long tunnel-hall with pegs, many round-windowed rooms on one level, bedrooms, bathrooms, cellars, pantries (lots), wardrobes, kitchens, dining-rooms; the Hill above with a tree and chimney pots.
- **S-03-01: Cutaway: the Last Homely House.** Cross-section of the elf-lord's house: hall of fire, kitchens and pantries, a library of maps, bedrooms, stables, terraces over the river.
- **S-04-01: Cutaway: under the mountains.** Cross-section of the goblin tunnels: the great cavern with its fire, forges and clanking machines, chains, winding passages, hidden back door.
- **S-05-01: Cutaway: down to the lake.** Cross-section of tunnels sloping down to the lake cavern and its island; dripping stone; the hobbit's path.
- **S-07-01: Cutaway: the great wooden hall.** Cross-section of the farmstead: long timber hall with central hearth and carved pillars, sleeping platform, barns, dairy, honey stores, stalls, hives in the garden.
- **S-09-01: Cutaway: halls and cellars.** Cross-section of the elf-king's caves: halls, dungeon cells, wine cellars, the trapdoor over the underground river, the water-gate. (Single-page style study made: plate_cellar_mechanism.)
- **S-10-01: Cutaway: the stilt town.** Cross-section of the lake town: houses on piles, walkways, market, warehouses, boats moored beneath, the Master's hall.
- **S-12-01: Cutaway: inside the Mountain.** Cross-section of the Mountain: secret door and tunnel, great treasure hall with the sleeping dragon, old dwarf halls, stairs, forges, the river and front gate.
- **S-19-01: Cutaway: the hobbit-hole set to rights.** Cross-section of the hobbit-hole put back in order: treasure chests in the cellar, a study with maps, the sword over the mantel, memoirs on the desk.
